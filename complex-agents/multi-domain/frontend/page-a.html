Common Questions

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot password" on the login page, or ask the voice assistant for help.

Where can I find my invoices?

Go to Settings → Billing → Invoice History.

How do I contact a human?

Start with the voice assistant — it can escalate to a live agent if needed.
